01 Department Overview

Ursine Relations is responsible for the administration of Bear Corp's bear workforce, including employee records, workplace conduct, scheduling, and general personnel matters.

All bears employed by Bear Corp are considered members of the corporate workforce, regardless of their department, temperament, or willingness to participate in meetings.

03 Employee Responsibilities

Bear employees are expected to maintain appropriate workplace conduct and cooperate with authorised corporate procedures.

  • Attend assigned meetings and scheduled activities.
  • Maintain an up-to-date employee record.
  • Observe departmental responsibilities.
  • Cooperate with applicable dental procedure schedules.
  • Report workplace disagreements through the appropriate channels.

04 Inter-Bear Relations

Bear Corp recognises that workplace relationships may occasionally become strained. Employees are encouraged to resolve minor disagreements professionally.

PERSONNEL GUIDANCE

Disagreements involving personal space, territorial behaviour, or the possession of unattended food should be referred to Ursine Relations.

05 Employee Conduct

The following behaviours are considered inconsistent with Bear Corp workplace standards:

  • Unprovoked growling during scheduled meetings.
  • Occupying another employee's assigned chair.
  • Withholding essential workplace information.
  • Refusing to identify oneself when requested.
  • Eating documents, regardless of their perceived nutritional value.

06 Dental Procedure Assignments

Bear employees may be assigned to participate in the company's Individual Tooth Licking programme in accordance with the applicable procedure schedule.

Assignment records are maintained separately from general personnel records. Refer to the BR-LICK-42 Operator's Manual for the relevant fictional procedure documentation.
