Corporate Services Division

Facilities & Workplace Operations

Office locations, equipment, access and facility procedures.

FACILITIES DIRECTORY Document: BR-FAC-001
Revision: 11/2026
Classification: Internal Use

01 Office Locations

Bear Corp maintains several designated workplace areas for the daily activities of its ursine workforce. Employees are expected to familiarise themselves with the location and intended use of each facility.

Primary workplace facilities
FacilityPurposeAccess
Main OfficeGeneral administration and bear operations.All employees
Dental Preparation RoomPreparation of personnel and equipment for scheduled tooth-cleaning duties.Authorised personnel
Employee BreakroomRest periods, refreshments, and informal workplace interaction.All employees
Records DepartmentStorage of employee files, dental records, and completed procedural documentation.Restricted
Facilities StorageCleaning supplies, replacement equipment, and general maintenance materials.Facilities staff

02 Workplace Equipment

Equipment must be returned to its designated storage location following use. Items should not be relocated between departments without authorisation.

  • Dental procedure stations
  • Employee identification boards
  • Approved cleaning and sanitation supplies
  • Protective equipment storage cabinets
  • Bear-sized seating and resting furniture
FACILITY NOTICE

Furniture displacement caused by bears sitting in unintended locations must be reported to Facilities. Employees should not attempt to reposition occupied furniture.

03 Access & Security

Employees must use their assigned access credentials when entering restricted areas. Tailgating, unauthorised room access and unexplained presence in the Records Department are prohibited.

Bear employees are not exempt from access requirements on the basis of size, seniority or apparent indifference.

04 Maintenance Requests

Damaged furniture, malfunctioning equipment, and general facility concerns should be reported to Facilities Support.

Reports should include the affected location, a description of the issue, and whether the issue presents an immediate workplace hazard.

05 Workplace Notices

  • Employees must not store personal food in dental preparation equipment.
  • Breakroom seating is allocated on a first-come basis, subject to bear dimensions.
  • Unidentified fur deposits should be reported to Facilities.
  • Employees are reminded that the office aquarium is not a drinking station.
