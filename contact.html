01 Department Directory

02 Support Requests

Support requests should include sufficient information to allow the receiving department to identify and assess the issue.

Where applicable, employees should provide:

  • Employee name and identification number.
  • Department and workplace location.
  • A brief description of the matter.
  • The date the issue was first observed.
  • Any relevant document or procedure reference.

03 Incident Reporting

Workplace incidents, procedural deviations and equipment failures should be reported promptly through the appropriate department.

IMMEDIATE ATTENTION REQUIRED

Incidents involving injury, unsafe conditions or uncontrolled bear behaviour must be escalated immediately. Do not delay reporting while attempting to determine which department is technically responsible.

04 Dental Procedure Enquiries

Questions concerning Individual Tooth Licking assignments, scheduling or procedural compliance should be directed to Dental Procedure Compliance.

Employees should consult the BR-LICK-42 Operator's Manual before submitting questions already addressed by the current documentation.

05 Frequently Asked Questions

Who do I contact if a bear refuses to attend a scheduled procedure?

Contact Ursine Relations. Repeated non-attendance should be recorded in the employee's personnel file.

Who do I contact if equipment is missing?

Contact Facilities Support. Please do not assume that another department has borrowed the equipment without permission.

Can I submit an anonymous complaint?

Yes. However, anonymous submissions may limit the department's ability to investigate the matter or provide a response.

What if I do not know which department to contact?

Contact General Administration. They will determine where your enquiry belongs. This may take several working days.

06 General Correspondence

All correspondence should be professional, relevant and appropriately addressed. Please avoid submitting multiple copies of the same request to different departments.

PLEASE NOTE

Bear Corp does not accept responsibility for enquiries submitted to the wrong department, placed in unattended food containers or communicated exclusively through growling.
