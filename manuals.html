02 Document Control

All corporate documents are assigned a unique reference number and revision date. Employees must ensure that printed or locally stored copies have not been superseded.

  • Current documents are identified by their latest revision date.
  • Obsolete documents must not be used for operational purposes.
  • Unauthorised amendments are prohibited.
  • Employees should report missing or damaged documentation.

03 Reading Requirements

Employees assigned to a procedure are expected to review the relevant documentation before commencing the activity.

DOCUMENT WARNING

Possession of a manual does not constitute evidence that the employee has read, understood or intends to comply with its contents.

04 Document Requests

Requests for new documentation, revisions,or replacement copies should be submitted through the Contact department.

Requests should include the document reference number, the nature of the requested change, and the reason the existing document is considered insufficient.
